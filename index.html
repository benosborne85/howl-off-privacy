Howl Off: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Howl Off ("the app") is a voice-controlled game. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Your microphone

The app uses your microphone to measure how loud you are and what pitch you are making, so your voice can control the game.

Audio is analysed on your device, in real time. It is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your progress (level, coins, unlocked monsters, settings, and your voice calibration) is stored locally on your device.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also reset it any time in Settings.

Online play and friends (Game Center)

Matchmaking, friends and online battles use Apple Game Center. If you play online, your Game Center display name and

in-game actions (such as how loud and how high your roar is, and which moves you use) are shared with your opponent through Apple's

servers for the length of the match. Only these numbers are shared, never your voice or any audio. Game Center is provided by Apple and is covered by

Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive or store this data.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email address required
  • No analytics of your play
  • No ads unless you choose to watch one (see below)
  • We do not sell or share personal information

Optional ads

Howl Off offers optional rewarded video ads (for example, to earn extra coins). You only see an ad if you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (from your IP address), and information about how you interact with ads and your device, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, ads may be more relevant to you; if you decline, ads still work but are not personalised. In the EU and UK we ask for your consent first. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Howl Off does not send your voice or audio to anyone, and does not give advertisers any of your game data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date.
